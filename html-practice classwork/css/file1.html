Internship form is a document that is used to collect information from students or candidates who are applying for an internship position. It typically includes fields for personal information, educational background, work experience, skills, and other relevant details. The form may also include questions about the candidate's availability, preferred internship duration, and any specific requirements or preferences they may have. Internship forms are often used by companies, organizations, and educational institutions to streamline the application process and gather necessary information from potential interns.
